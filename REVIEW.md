# Adversarial self-review (round 1) and revisions

The review is written as a hostile A* reviewer would write it. Each point lists the problem, how
severe it is, and what was changed in the code or the paper. Points marked **OPEN** need real GPU
results before they can be closed.

## Novelty

**R1. "HGCD is CAD with a different negative context."** *Major.*
The decoding rule has the same algebraic form as CAD/CFG. The novelty has to come from three
things: (i) the negative is a graph-aware intervention on the model's *own* chain, with the
context held fixed; (ii) theory that separates direct from total effects and explains why CAD
picks up distractor paths; (iii) a protocol with known ground truth.
*Changes:* Related work now states exactly what CAD, AdaCAD, NWCAD, Pced and RED do and do not
do. CAD-chain (chain vs. no chain, i.e. a text analogue of RED) is a required baseline, so the
gain from the *graph* is isolated by `hgcd` vs `cad_chain` and `hgcd` vs `hgcd_uniform`.

**R2. The propositions are algebraically trivial.** *Moderate.*
That is true, and intended. They are design rationales that produce *falsifiable predictions*
(naive masking near chance on bridge hops; CAD hurt more by distractors; placebo contrast equals
CoT), not deep theorems.
*Changes:* The paper says so explicitly in the Theory section and in the Limitations section.
Every proposition is paired with a prediction and the table that tests it.

## Validity of the causal claims

**R3. `[MASKED]` is out of distribution, so Δ may measure a mask-token artifact.** *Major.*
*Changes:* The redundant hop in the estimator benchmark is masked in exactly the same way as the
necessary hops, so a pure mask artifact shows up as Δ>0 on redundant hops and lowers the AUC.
This is now stated as a control in the Setup section. A content-preserving placebo is also
reported for every hop.

**R4. "Causal" overclaim: an LLM is not an SCM.** *Moderate.*
*Changes:* The Setup section now says that editing the prompt is an exact intervention on the
reader's input, so Δ is an exact effect *on the model*. The task SCM supplies only ground truth
(which hops are necessary, which input changes should have no effect).

**R5. The rollout-free `do_total` may not approximate the real do().** *Moderate, OPEN.*
*Changes:* Rollout PNS (Yu et al.-style) is implemented (`est_rollouts>0`; on in the
`kaggle_full` and `a100` presets) and reported next to Δ^total.

## Experimental design

**R6. No compute-matched baseline.** *Major.*
*Changes:* Added self-consistency with 5 sampled chains (`sc`). It uses strictly more compute
than HGCD, which generates nothing extra.

**R7. Statistical power.** *Major, OPEN.*
With 40 questions per k, a 95% CI on accuracy is about ±9 points per k and about ±6 points per
split.
*Changes:* The paper declares two primary comparisons (HGCD vs CoT and HGCD vs CAD-chain on OOD
CLUTRR and on MuSiQue) and reports paired tests. `kaggle_full` (80 per k, 300 MuSiQue questions)
is recommended for the paper numbers; `a100` uses 100 per k.

**R8. α tuned on test.** *Avoided by design.* α is selected on the CLUTRR validation split and
frozen. The sweep figure uses validation data only.

**R9. The DRG built by string matching breaks on pronouns.** *Moderate.*
*Changes:* Records now store `drg_connected`. The summary reports format success, hop fidelity
against the gold path, DRG connectivity and mean number of hops. The Limitations section
discusses the failure mode.

**R10. Closed-set scoring flatters CoT or HGCD.** *Minor.*
All methods are scored over the same candidate set (20 relations on CLUTRR; on MuSiQue, model
strings plus paragraph titles, with candidate recall reported). The paper now says this
explicitly.

**R11. Interventions generated by an LLM would be biased (the CausalDANN limitation).** *Avoided.*
All interventions are programmatic and seeded, with known effects. The only model calls are the
reader itself.

**R12. CLUTRR label noise.** *Minor.*
Stories whose gold graph has back-edges include known label ambiguities, for example a wife's
father labelled "father". We keep simple-path stories only and report the filter counts.

## Code review findings fixed

- Rollout PNS rebuilt the corrupted hop with a different RNG than the one used in the prompt.
  Fixed: the corrupted hops are computed once and reused.
- MuSiQue `is_final` used a convoluted expression. Simplified: the redundant hop is never last
  by construction.
- `pkill -f` pattern matching its own shell (tooling only).
- L1 now defaults to probability-space contrast, consistent with Prop. 2; `space='log'` is
  available as an ablation.

## Still open after round 1

- R5/R7 need the Kaggle/A100 runs.
- Free-generation accuracy for CLUTRR (without candidate scoring) is not reported. Low priority,
  since all methods share the scorer.
- (Closed in round 1b) Added a **mask-marker placebo**: `[MASKED]` is inserted into hop j with all
  of its content kept. Δ^marker isolates the cost of the mask token itself and is reported in the
  estimator tables next to Δ^placebo.
