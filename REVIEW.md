# Adversarial self-review (round 1) and revisions

The review is written as a hostile A* reviewer would write it. Each point lists the problem, how
severe it is, and what was changed in the code or the paper. Points marked **OPEN** need real GPU
results before they can be closed.

## Novelty

**R1. "HGCD is CAD with a different negative context."** *Major.*
The decoding rule has the same algebraic form as CAD/CFG. The novelty has to come from three
things: (i) the negative is a graph-aware intervention on the model's *own* chain, with the
context held fixed; (ii) theory that separates direct from total effects and explains why CAD
picks up distractor paths; (iii) a protocol with known ground truth.
*Changes:* Related work now states exactly what CAD, AdaCAD, NWCAD, Pced and RED do and do not
do. CAD-chain (chain vs. no chain, i.e. a text analogue of RED) is a required baseline, so the
gain from the *graph* is isolated by `hgcd` vs `cad_chain` and `hgcd` vs `hgcd_uniform`.

**R2. The propositions are algebraically trivial.** *Moderate.*
That is true, and intended. They are design rationales that produce *falsifiable predictions*
(naive masking near chance on bridge hops; CAD hurt more by distractors; placebo contrast equals
CoT), not deep theorems.
*Changes:* The paper says so explicitly in the Theory section and in the Limitations section.
Every proposition is paired with a prediction and the table that tests it.

## Validity of the causal claims

**R3. `[MASKED]` is out of distribution, so Δ may measure a mask-token artifact.** *Major.*
*Changes:* The redundant hop in the estimator benchmark is masked in exactly the same way as the
necessary hops, so a pure mask artifact shows up as Δ>0 on redundant hops and lowers the AUC.
This is now stated as a control in the Setup section. A content-preserving placebo is also
reported for every hop.

**R4. "Causal" overclaim: an LLM is not an SCM.** *Moderate.*
*Changes:* The Setup section now says that editing the prompt is an exact intervention on the
reader's input, so Δ is an exact effect *on the model*. The task SCM supplies only ground truth
(which hops are necessary, which input changes should have no effect).

**R5. The rollout-free `do_total` may not approximate the real do().** *Moderate, OPEN.*
*Changes:* Rollout PNS (Yu et al.-style) is implemented (`est_rollouts>0`; on in the
`kaggle_full` and `a100` presets) and reported next to Δ^total.

## Experimental design

**R6. No compute-matched baseline.** *Major.*
*Changes:* Added self-consistency with 5 sampled chains (`sc`). It uses strictly more compute
than HGCD, which generates nothing extra.

**R7. Statistical power.** *Major, OPEN.*
With 40 questions per k, a 95% CI on accuracy is about ±9 points per k and about ±6 points per
split.
*Changes:* The paper declares two primary comparisons (HGCD vs CoT and HGCD vs CAD-chain on OOD
CLUTRR and on MuSiQue) and reports paired tests. `kaggle_full` (80 per k, 300 MuSiQue questions)
is recommended for the paper numbers; `a100` uses 100 per k.

**R8. α tuned on test.** *Avoided by design.* α is selected on the CLUTRR validation split and
frozen. The sweep figure uses validation data only.

**R9. The DRG built by string matching breaks on pronouns.** *Moderate.*
*Changes:* Records now store `drg_connected`. The summary reports format success, hop fidelity
against the gold path, DRG connectivity and mean number of hops. The Limitations section
discusses the failure mode.

**R10. Closed-set scoring flatters CoT or HGCD.** *Minor.*
All methods are scored over the same candidate set (20 relations on CLUTRR; on MuSiQue, model
strings plus paragraph titles, with candidate recall reported). The paper now says this
explicitly.

**R11. Interventions generated by an LLM would be biased (the CausalDANN limitation).** *Avoided.*
All interventions are programmatic and seeded, with known effects. The only model calls are the
reader itself.

**R12. CLUTRR label noise.** *Minor.*
Stories whose gold graph has back-edges include known label ambiguities, for example a wife's
father labelled "father". We keep simple-path stories only and report the filter counts.

## Code review findings fixed

- Rollout PNS rebuilt the corrupted hop with a different RNG than the one used in the prompt.
  Fixed: the corrupted hops are computed once and reused.
- MuSiQue `is_final` used a convoluted expression. Simplified: the redundant hop is never last
  by construction.
- `pkill -f` pattern matching its own shell (tooling only).
- L1 now defaults to probability-space contrast, consistent with Prop. 2; `space='log'` is
  available as an ablation.

## Still open after round 1

- R5/R7 need the Kaggle/A100 runs.
- Free-generation accuracy for CLUTRR (without candidate scoring) is not reported. Low priority,
  since all methods share the scorer.
- (Closed in round 1b) Added a **mask-marker placebo**: `[MASKED]` is inserted into hop j with all
  of its content kept. Δ^marker isolates the cost of the mask token itself and is reported in the
  estimator tables next to Δ^placebo.

## Round 2–3 (after running the full pipeline and the notebook on CPU)

The pipeline ran end to end twice on CPU with Qwen2.5-0.5B: once via the CLI (37 min) and once
via **Run All on the notebook** with nbclient (41 min, no cell errors). Findings and fixes:

**R13. Placebo contrast moves decisions.** *Major (design).* At α=1 the placebo contrast already
changed answers in the pilot (CLUTRR n=12: 33% vs CoT 17%). So "contrast against any
perturbation" is not neutral.
*Changes:* HGCD vs placebo-contrast is now a **primary** comparison, and the ablation hypothesis
states that the placebo contrast, not CoT, is the control.

**R14. HGCD cannot fix a wrong chain.** *Major (scope).* L2 makes the answer depend more on the
chain, so it only repairs readout errors.
*Changes:* Added Remark "What each level can fix", the Readout hypothesis, and
`tab_clutrr_readout.tex`, which splits accuracy by whether the chain reaches every gold-path
entity.

**R15. MuSiQue candidate recall was 17% (pilot), which capped every method.** *Major.*
*Changes:* Candidates are now model strings + titles + capitalised spans + dates/numbers from the
context, with sentence-starter stopwords removed, capped at 64. Gold recall measured offline on
600 dev questions is 56% before the model strings are added (titles alone: 10.5%). Scoring is
chunked over a copied KV cache (verified against full recomputation, max error 6e-5).

**R16. α ties at small n select α=0, so HGCD = CoT.** *Expected.* Ties are broken toward the
smaller α (conservative, documented). With the `kaggle` dev size (60) ties are much rarer.

**R17. Kaggle sessions can die mid-run.** *Operational.*
*Changes:* Stage-level checkpoints (`stage_*.pkl`, `*_records.pkl`); rerunning the notebook
resumes. Unit-tested.

**R18. `transformers` versions: older ones may ignore `dtype=` and load fp32.**
*Changes:* The keyword is chosen by version and the dtype is enforced after loading.

**R19. Self-consistency missing from the final tables** (no α entry from the dev run). Fixed.

**Pilot signal (illustration only, n≈20 hops).** On MuSiQue non-final gold hops,
Δ^naive = −0.04 while Δ^total = 0.42, which is the pattern Prop. 1 predicts. Placebo and
mask-marker Δ stay within ±0.08. CLUTRR effects at 0.5B are small (the model rarely composes
relations correctly: hop fidelity 0.56, DRG connected 61%).

### Literature check: novelty must be narrowed
- **FRODO (EMNLP-F 2024)** already treats the rationale as a mediator and finds weak indirect
  effects under 20B parameters. "Small models don't use their chains" is **not new**.
- **Thought Branches (ICLR 2026)** already argues that step effects must be measured by
  resampling (a total effect) and notes that removing a sentence while blocking its reappearance
  changes conclusions. The **direct-vs-total distinction is not new** in spirit.
- **Breaking the Chain (arXiv 2026)** shows models often fail to update after edits to
  intermediate structures.
- What remains specific to this project: (i) **known-by-construction ground truth** for necessity
  and for null effects, so estimators can be *validated* rather than compared with each other;
  (ii) a deterministic, rollout-free total-effect intervention for hop chains, validated against
  resampling; (iii) the bridge-vs-compositional prediction of *where* direct estimators fail
  (confirmed: Δnaive 0.004 vs Δtot 0.639); (iv) an exact NDE/NIE mediation of distractor effects.

### Decision: continue, but pivot
- **Drop** "HGCD improves accuracy" as the headline. Keep HGCD as a probe and report the negative
  result with its causal explanation: readout decoding can only amplify dependence that exists.
- **New headline:** a ground-truth benchmark for step-importance estimators across model scale and
  task-graph type, plus mediation of robustness failures. This is an analysis paper, the same
  kind as Stolfo (ACL 2023), FUR (EMNLP 2025) and Thought Branches (ICLR 2026).
- **Realistic venue:** ACL/EMNLP Findings, or main only if the scale study shows a clear pattern
  (e.g. CLUTRR reliance switching on between 3B and 7B while direct estimators keep failing on
  bridge hops at every scale).

### Changes made in this round
- Added estimators: counterfactual replacement (direct / propagated) and rollout PNS on MuSiQue.
  The benchmark now compares 5 estimators and 2 controls against ground truth.
- Added `reliance` preset and notebook mode: Qwen2.5 0.5/1.5/3/7B + SmolLM2 over 2 GPUs (7B
  sharded with `device_map=auto`), aggregated into `tab_reliance.tex`.
- Added exact mediation (NDE with the original chain fed under the intervened input; NIE = TE −

- **Bug: SmolLM2 ran out of GPU memory at the end of round 1.** It has no grouped-query attention,

  generation, and `expandable_segments`.

### Round 4b: a design flaw caught by the micro run
Counterfactual replacement "with propagation" first only *renamed* the bridge entity in
descendant hops. On MuSiQue non-final hops it gave Δ = 0.02, the same as the direct version,
because the last hop still stated the answer. Renaming is not an intervention on downstream
**values**. Fixed: propagation now also invalidates the descendants' results. After the fix
(3 questions, CPU, illustration only): cf-total 0.44 vs cf-direct 0.01 on non-final hops,
matching mask-total 0.35 vs mask-direct 0.001. This lesson goes in the paper: any deterministic
"total effect" estimator must invalidate descendant values, not just rename mentions.

## Round 5: insights mined from round 1 (no new GPU run)
- **I1:** 99.4% of a non-final bridge hop's effect is indirect (0.004 vs 0.639). Step-ablation
  faithfulness metrics would call necessary bridge steps unnecessary.
- **I2 (copy, not compute):** reliance depends on whether the answer is a *string in the chain*
  (MuSiQue final-hop effect 0.68) rather than a value that must be composed (CLUTRR ≈ 0). New
  binary intervention T_copy: append a hop that writes the answer. CPU check (0.5B, n=4,
  illustration only): content effect 0.003 → 0.59, and CLUTRR switches to the bridge pattern.
- **I3 (faithfulness amplifies failures):** distractors cost chain-based methods 7.1 EM vs 1.6 for
  chain-free ones; direct beats CoT (25.0 vs 20.8 EM) at 1.5B.
- **I4:** decoding can only amplify existing dependence. The null gains at both reliance extremes
  are predicted, not accidental.
- **New in code:** T_copy estimator variant (`clutrr_copy`), form-vs-content decomposition
  (masked chain of the same shape vs no chain), and a Direct×CoT product-of-experts reader.

## Round 6: decoding run (Qwen2.5-1.5B + SmolLM2-1.7B, 269 min each, both completed)
Replicated across two model families (`results/decoding/tab_replication.tex`):
- **F1, copy not compute (H11 ✔):** chain content effect CLUTRR 0.8 / 2.0 pts → 75.1 / 62.8 with
  T_copy; MuSiQue 56.4 / 71.9. Chains that reach every gold-path entity do not raise CLUTRR
  accuracy (17.1 vs 18.3; 15.2 vs 22.4). Form effect ≈ 0 (H-form ✘).
- **F2, direct-effect blind spot (H1 ✔):** non-final hops Δtot 66–76 vs Δnaive ≤ 1.3 pts; AUC
  total 0.975–1.000 vs direct 0.64–0.83; two total-effect implementations agree; controls at chance.
- **F3, mediation (H6 ✔):** dist8 hurts CoT entirely through the regenerated chain (NIE −10.0 /
  −9.2; NDE +0.8 / +1.7); direct answering is hurt at the readout (NDE −4.2 / −6.7).
  Prop. 3 (CAD more fragile at readout) ✘.
- **F4 (H2 ✘, H8 ✔):** HGCD = CoT everywhere. Only content-free calibration beats CoT on CLUTRR
  (+5.9, McNemar p = 0.009 vs HGCD; +3.4 n.s.) and it is fragile under the neutral placebo
  (SmolLM2 −5.0 [−10.1, −0.4]).

**Reviewer objection to F1:** "Writing the answer into the chain obviously makes the model use it."
*Response, added to the code:* the decisive test is a WRONG answer hop that contradicts the
relational hops (`clutrr_copywrong`). A computing reader would resist it; a copying reader follows
it. CPU check (0.5B, n=2, illustration only): followed in 2/2, p(gold) → 0.001–0.007. This is H12
for round 3, together with a copy diagnostic on the model's own chains (does the chosen relation
appear as a word in the chain?).

**Paper reframed** around F1–F4 ("Read, Not Reasoned"); HGCD kept as the decoding probe whose null
result is explained by F1 and F3. Remaining open question for A*: does computation over the chain
emerge with scale (`MODE="reliance"`, 0.5B → 7B)?

## Round 9 — decoding with causal/structural information (hgcd/causaldec.py)
Survey: `reports/Giải mã nhân quả đa bước.md`. Pre-registered go/no-go: beat max(cot_free, sc) by
≥ 2 points with 95% CI > 0 on both Qwen2.5-1.5B and 3B.

**C (chain-order symbolic composition), CLUTRR k ∈ {2,3,4,6,8}, n = 278.** NO-GO. sym_sc − sc:
+1.8 [0.0, 3.6] (1.5B), +2.2 [0.4, 4.3] (3B), +2.5 [0.7, 4.7] (7B). Diagnosis: the rule table
(induced from training stories only; 95% correct on gold chains) applies to only 4–6% of model
chains: models list facts out of order, invert them, or write non-relational sentences.
Order-free graph composition is accurate when a path exists (48% vs 24% CoT at 3B; 63% vs 33% at
7B) but covers 8–25% of chains → bottleneck = fact extraction, not composition.

**C2 (fact extraction → kinship graph → rule table), same 278 questions.** Passes the threshold:
ext_sc − sc = +6.1 [2.5, 10.1] (1.5B, p = .003), +7.2 [3.2, 11.2] (3B, p = .001),
+7.6 [4.0, 11.2] (7B, p < .001); ext_all +8.3 / +8.6 at 3B / 7B. Gains concentrate at k = 2–3.
**Caveats (why this is not yet a GO):** (i) the method was designed after looking at C on the same
questions → needs held-out confirmation; (ii) ext_sc falls back to sc when no extraction
composes (≈ 80% of questions), so its compute ≈ 5 chains + 5 extractions > sc (5 chains).

**C3 (pre-registered confirmation, launched before seeing any result):** held-out chain lengths
k ∈ {5, 7, 9, 10} (never evaluated before), seed 1, Qwen 1.5B/3B/7B, plus a compute-matched
baseline sc_plus = self-consistency over 10 sampled chains. Primary endpoint: ext_sc − max(cot_free,

## Round 10 (pre-registered before any GPU run): reading-friendly chains, trained

**Motivation.** R3/D3: models copy what is written in the chain but do not compose k relations in one
step; a rule table composes correctly. Decoding cannot fix this. If instead *every step* of the chain is
a pairwise composition ("running relation"), each step is a one-step lookup, which models can do, and
the final answer is a copy of the last line. We test whether a LoRA-trained model learns this and
whether it generalises beyond the training lengths. No rule table at inference time.

**Pilot (offline, existing per-hop data, results/reliance_v2).** CF-DAPO's R_nec = mean max(0, Δ) of
non-final gold hops. With the proposal's single-hop masking (do_naive): 1.0-8.9 points on every model and
dataset (MuSiQue 1.0-8.9, CLUTRR 1.9-6.6): the reward is empty exactly where credit assignment is needed.
With do_total: 42-77 points on MuSiQue and on CLUTRR+copy (answer written in the chain: a copy hack), but
only 3.1-10.6 on plain CLUTRR. So (i) R_nec must use do_total, (ii) it rewards copying an answer written
in a hop, (iii) on compositional chains the reward is sparse for untrained models. Consequence: start with
supervised structure (this round); RL with a do_total reward only if this round shows signal.

**Arms** (Qwen2.5-1.5B and 3B-Instruct, LoRA r=16 on all linear layers, fp16, 1 epoch, 3000 CLUTRR
train stories with k in {2,3,4} from gen_train234, zero-shot system prompt, loss on the answer only):
* B0  base, few-shot plain CoT (existing decisions.csv: cot_free, sc_plus; same uids)
* P1  base, few-shot *structured* CoT (prompting only, no training)
* S_plain   SFT on plain gold chains ("Hop i: X's rel is Y. => Y" ... Answer)
* S_struct  SFT on structured chains: after each hop a running-composition line
            "So Y is <source>'s <c_i>." with c_i from the pairwise rule table induced on the TRAIN split
            (examples whose stepwise composition disagrees with the gold answer are dropped)
* S_struct+DPO  only if S_struct passes the primary endpoint (separately pre-registered below)

**Evaluation sets** (identical sampling to rounds 9 so baselines join on uid):
C4 validation k=2-4 (seed 2, 100/k), C6 unseen task k=2-4 (seed 7, 60/k), C5 unseen task k=5-8
(seed 5, 50/k). Train k never exceeds 4, so C5 tests length generalisation.
Robustness: on the C6 questions, rename / order / distract (true effect 0); report accuracy and the
flip rate (answer differs from the unperturbed answer of the same model).

**Primary endpoint.** C5 (k=5-8): accuracy(S_struct, greedy) minus max(accuracy S_plain, B0 cot_free,
B0 sc_plus), paired bootstrap 95% CI > 0 and point estimate >= +2 points, at Qwen2.5-3B. 1.5B is reported
regardless. GO = 3B passes. NO-GO otherwise: then structure adds nothing beyond plain SFT on the same data
and we report that.
**Secondary.** Same contrast on C4 and C6; S_struct vs S_plain flip rate under rename/order/distract
(lower is better); S_struct vs D2 extract+rules (ext_g_sc) is exploratory.
**Declared limits.** SFT uses train gold chains (labels the base CoT never saw); the claim is about what
chain structure adds *given the same supervision*, hence the S_plain control. One seed per arm.

## Round 11 (pre-registered before any GPU run): causal training methods, scale, natural text

**Why.** Round 10 / 10b: fine-tuning on gold chains is the main lever (CLUTRR 3B 36 -> 95% val; 2Wiki +21..+30 EM);
structure and citations add nothing. Open: does the chain matter after fine-tuning, does the model *use* it,
is it robust, can RL / counterfactual preference training improve it, does it scale and transfer?

**Literature consulted (search snippets/abstracts only; numbers not verified against the papers).**
FRODO (Paul et al., EMNLP 2024 Findings: DPO on counterfactual rationales + indirect-effect / margin / counterfactual
losses; reports +2-3 accuracy and +4.5 robustness over SFT), interchange-intervention training and causal
distillation (Geiger et al. ICML 2022; Wu et al. NAACL 2022), step-necessity rewards for faithful CoT (CoSR,
arXiv 2603.22816, which also cites RLVR lowering faithfulness), CASE / counterfactual simulation training /
information-flow faithfulness (2026 preprints), DISCO and dually self-improved counterfactual augmentation,
GRPO/DAPO (token-level loss, dynamic sampling), "GRPO is secretly a process reward model", LoRA variants
(DoRA, PiSSA, rsLoRA; a 2026 paper shows batch size alone flips their rankings, so we keep plain LoRA r=16 and
fixed batch/lr). Not implemented: interchange-intervention training (needs internal alignment search), CoSR-style
ablation reward (planned if GRPO shows signal).

**Arms and implementation** (hgcd/cfrl.py, hgcd/egen.py; Qwen2.5, LoRA r=16, 3000 CLUTRR chains k<=4):
* s_ans   SFT on the answer only.            * s_plain SFT on gold chains (round-10 control).
* s_dpo   SFT, then DPO (beta 0.1, lr 2e-5, 1 epoch, 1500 pairs, +0.2 NLL on chosen) with programmatic counterfactual
          rejected chains: 40% gold hops + a hop stating a WRONG answer (the T_copy-wrong form), 30% one corrupted
          hop with a consistent wrong answer, 30% right hops + wrong answer. Reference = the SFT model.
* s_grpo  SFT, then GRPO (P=8 prompts x G=8 samples, T=1, lr 2e-5, <=200 steps / 150 min, token-level loss, no KL,
          dynamic sampling of groups with reward variance) with reward = correct answer + 0.3 x hop fidelity
          (fraction of generated hops that are gold triples) - 0.2 if no "Answer:" line.
* 7B: s_plain and s_ans, fp16 split over 2 GPUs (batch 1 x accum 16).
* MuSiQue (4 arms, round-10b design) and 2Wiki type shift (train compositional only, test inference only).
Extra causal measurements on every chain-writing model (120 unseen k=2-4 questions): wrong-copy follow rate
(round-3 protocol, scored over the relation set) overall and given PS=1, and the mean do_total effect of non-final
gold hops on p(gold).

  the stage-1 SFT model of the same run, AND accuracy on C5 and C6 does not fall by more than 2 points (lower CI bound
